privacy

This site is a home for one exhibition. It doesn't have accounts, ads or tracking, and it doesn't sell or share anything about you.

what this site collects

Nothing on its own. There are no analytics, no tracking pixels and no cookies set by this site.

services the site uses

A few outside services make the site work, and they have their own policies:

  • Hosting. The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep basic server logs, such as IP addresses, for security. GitHub privacy statement
  • Fonts. The typefaces load from Google Fonts, so your browser contacts Google to download them. Google privacy policy
  • Music. The soundtrack player is embedded from Spotify and may set Spotify cookies when it loads or plays. Spotify privacy policy

the mailing list

The sign-up form isn't connected yet, so it doesn't store anything you type. Once it is, your email will only be used to tell you about new tapes and shows, and every email will have a link to unsubscribe.
